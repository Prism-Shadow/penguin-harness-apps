# Changelog

Release tags group the plugin versions listed in each section. A GitHub prerelease uses `vYYYY.MM.DD-rc.N` and attaches the installable tarballs and their SHA-512 catalog.

## [2026.11.23-rc.10]

### @penguinharness/penguin-snowboard 0.2.0-rc.1

Charge a jump on the snow itself or with Space. Brake or tuck on the ground and turn in the air. Follow numbered flags and carved tracks down the ridge, then retry the course from the finish.

### @penguinharness/midnight-rhythm 0.2.0-rc.2

Play the new Encore arrangement. Match the arrows on the pads and notes, and follow the ring to hold a note through its end. Letting go early gives feedback at the gate.

### @penguinharness/pocket-arcade 0.2.0-rc.1

Deliver six rooftop stamps in Rooftop Post, or queue your next turn in Canal Courier. Each cabinet keeps its own best score, and your mute choice carries across all ten games.

## [2026.11.23-rc.9]

### @penguinharness/continual-learning 0.2.19-rc.2

A product that ships this package installs its learning hook on newly provisioned default Agents. Other Agents can install it individually.

## [2026.11.23-rc.8]

### @penguinharness/character-chat 0.2.21

Use the speaker beside a character’s reply to play it with the Project’s speech model. Pause or stop playback, or choose a different reply, including replies from earlier chapters. Requires the multimodal tools plugin with transient speech playback.

## [2026.11.23-rc.7]

Midnight Rhythm, Frostline, Pocket Arcade and Worlds Anthems can be installed individually on an Agent. Their tarballs can also be transferred to an offline machine.

### @penguinharness/midnight-rhythm 0.2.0-rc.1

Choose the Night ride or its syncopated Encore chart. Strike the upper or lower lane as a note crosses the gate, and hold ribbon notes to the end. Each of the four stations has its own phrase. The results show your grade, accuracy and best score for the route.

### @penguinharness/penguin-snowboard 0.1.0-rc.2

Jump and land cleanly inside two marked snow zones to complete the downhill challenge. The course shows the distance to the next zone. The finish flag counts the zones you landed in, and the touch controls fit inside compact workbench windows.

### @penguinharness/pocket-arcade 0.1.0-rc.2

Replay the current Tape Loop phrase, then tap back its notes. Each pad has its own sound. The ten cabinets support pausing and return you to the game you just played in the arcade index.

### @penguinharness/worlds-anthems 0.1.0-rc.2

Play, pause and scrub the original instrumentals Northbound and Afterglow offline. The official Worlds tracks still stream from SoundCloud. Each playlist advances when a track ends, and Repeat playlist starts it again after the last track.

Transport Ship includes the original game and its sound. Open the workbench and press Play, even on the first launch without an internet connection. If you previously saved a personal copy, you can still open it from the game toolbar.

## [2026.11.23-rc.4]

### @penguinharness/harbor-pulse 1.3.0

Transport Ship saves the verified original game from its author on first launch and plays locally with sound on subsequent visits. Previously imported games open automatically. To start on a computer without internet access, prepare a private workbench ZIP on a connected computer.

## [2026.11.23-rc.3]

Study Map and Midnight Rhythm are optional workbenches. Choose their tarballs below for manual installation. The `packages.json` attachment lists the SHA-512 integrity of each package.

### @penguinharness/midnight-rhythm 0.1.0-rc.2

Press up to send the penguin into the air or down to crouch on the sled, even between notes. Follow the midnight train along the two lanes. The star gate marks a successful hit, and reduced motion keeps the lane changes while holding the scenery still.

### @penguinharness/study-map 1.2.0

Study Map opens with a field for notes, a passage or a public GitHub repository. Choose an example below it or reopen a saved study at its last selected node.

## [2026.11.23-rc.2]

Four optional workbenches join the GitHub release catalog. Choose a game or music player below and install its tarball from the release attachments. The `packages.json` attachment lists the package name, version, filename and SHA-512 digest for every workbench.

### @penguinharness/pocket-arcade 0.1.0-rc.1

Pocket Arcade adds ten original games with touch and keyboard controls. Open the cartridge shelf in a workbench, or play individual games offline from their standalone HTML pages.

### @penguinharness/worlds-anthems 0.1.0-rc.1

Worlds Anthems is an optional player for eight League of Legends Worlds tracks. Its season playlist drives the official SoundCloud widget, with transport, volume, seeking and direct links to each recording.

### @penguinharness/midnight-rhythm 0.1.0-rc.1

Midnight Rhythm adds an optional two-lane penguin rhythm game with the original instrumental track *Last Train After Midnight*. Play with the keyboard or touch pads, pause and resume, and replay the track after the scorecard.

### @penguinharness/penguin-snowboard 0.1.0-rc.1

Frostline is an optional downhill snowboarding game with original Antarctic scenery, keyboard and touch controls, scored aerial tricks, and recoverable wipeouts.

## [2026.11.23-rc.1]

This first GitHub prerelease includes the optional workbench packages below. Each tarball matches the SHA-512 digest in the attached `packages.json`.

### @penguinharness/harbor-pulse 1.2.0

Transport Ship plays in one click. The workbench opens the author’s own online game, with mouse capture, and still contains no game code. When the game’s website cannot be reached, the workbench says so and offers to try again, open the game in the browser, or play offline. Playing offline from a local game file moves behind the **Play offline: choose a local file** link, and a remembered file opens with **Play offline**.

### @penguinharness/kaoyan-flashcards 0.2.20

During a quiz, Enter and Space activate the focused answer or toolbar button. Filters and Import keep keyboard input while open. Escape closes the panel and returns focus to the control that opened it.

### @penguinharness/study-map 1.1.0

- Source lines are readable inside PenguinHarness, and the page and its diagram follow the app's light or dark look instead of only the system's.
- The example says what to do first, and **Import** is the main button. **Import** also takes pasted text.
- A failed import reopens the dialog with what you entered and the reason. Pressing Enter in the address field no longer reopens the dialog over the import. Addresses without `https://`, ending in `.git` or pointing into the tree are accepted.
- The note about files left out appears only when readable files did not fit. A complete study shows its file count.
- Documents wrap in the source pane and their nodes are described as sections. AI and error messages use everyday words and name the Models page. **Remove** sits beside the **Studies** list.

### @penguinharness/english-workbench 0.3.0

- Annotated words are boxed in the text as the reader promises, including inflected forms and phrases, and any word of a boxed phrase opens the phrase's note.
- An empty library offers **Start reading**, and an empty review links back to reading and says when the next word comes up.
- A browser without an English voice says so once, instead of asking you to click again.
- The lesson picture on **Today** is shaded in dark mode.

### @penguinharness/perler-studio 0.2.20

The legend has separate buttons to highlight a colour and replace its beads. The replacement button stays visible on touchscreens. After a swap, keyboard focus moves to the replacement colour.
