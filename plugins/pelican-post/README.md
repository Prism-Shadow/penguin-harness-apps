# Pelican Post

Cycle the boardwalk, collect wind-blown letters and deliver them to seaside postboxes.

Install the plugin on your Agent, then open Pelican Post in the App Center.

Move with A/D or the arrows. Tap or press Space to jump for high letters and clear crates. Riding past a red postbox delivers the whole satchel. Pause with Escape. Sound and the high score are saved in this browser.

The game design, canvas art and audio synthesis are original to this showcase. The starting idea came from [pelican-bike in riba2534’s three-game demo](https://github.com/riba2534/claude-opus-5-5-demo/tree/54adc1fb64155f9dd7c1c90ecae7b8185ac540b8/pelican-bike).
