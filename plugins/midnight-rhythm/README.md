# Midnight Rhythm

A penguin follows a night train over the city in time with an original instrumental track. Hit the mint notes on the upper lane to fly and the apricot notes on the lower lane to ride the sled. Missing a note breaks the combo and the song continues.

Open the installed workbench on an Agent, or open `workbenches/midnight-rhythm/ui/index.html` directly in a browser. Press F or ↑ for the upper lane and J or ↓ for the lower lane, or tap the two pads. Space pauses and resumes. The music starts with the Start button. No network or account is needed to play.

Pressing the upper pad sends the penguin into the air immediately. The lower pad puts the penguin into a crouch on the sled, even between notes. The star gate flashes when a note scores. Reduced motion keeps the lane changes and turns off the scenery movement and impact trails.

Choose Night ride or Encore. Night ride plays Last Train After Midnight. Encore plays Echoes on the Bridge with a new melody and syncopated fills. The arrows on the notes match the pads. A ring fills while you sustain a note. Hold a ribbon to its end for 80 extra points. Off-beat presses break the combo and cost 25 points. Finish at 65% accuracy to clear the route, then try for an S at 95%. The results show the best score for each route where browser storage is available.
