# Frostline

Tap or hold the snow and release to jump. In the air, hold either half of the scene to turn. On the ground, the left arrow brakes and the right arrow tucks for speed. The numbered gate flags and snow marks show where a landing counts.
Land a player-initiated jump in two different orange zones to clear the trail. Each zone can earn one stamp. The result distinguishes a cleared challenge from reaching the finish without it.
Take a penguin down a 560-metre Antarctic ridge. Hold Space (or the centre touch control) to crouch and release to jump. A/D or the left and right arrows turn the board in the air. Release the turn early enough to match the next slope. A clean landing earns points for height, time in the air and full rotations. A wipeout costs speed, then the penguin gets back on the board.

Choose **Pause** or press P to take a break. **Ride again** restarts the course. The sound button or M mutes the synthesized audio. Score records and sound preferences stay in the browser. The original drawings, physics and sound run offline after installation.

Install the optional plugin in the library, add its workbench to an Agent and open **Frostline**. [中文](README.zh.md)

To play an unpacked copy offline, open [the course](workbenches/penguin-snowboard/ui/index.html) in your browser. For development, run `npm ci` and `node scripts/build-frostline.mjs` from the repository root after editing the game.
