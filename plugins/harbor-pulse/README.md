# Transport Ship

Play the original Transport Ship on its 3D cargo deck. Play offline from the first launch.

Open **Transport Ship** in your Agent’s App Center. WASD to move, mouse to look, click to play. Press Esc to pause.

The game and its sound controls are included in the installation. You can open a previously saved game from the toolbar. See the [source and dependency notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt).
