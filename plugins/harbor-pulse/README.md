# Transport Ship

Play Transport Ship on the 3D cargo deck in one click. The game opens from its author’s website, and you can play your own copy offline.

Open **Transport Ship** in your Agent’s App Center. WASD to move, mouse to look, click to play. Press Esc to pause.

The online game needs an internet connection. To play offline, choose **Play offline: choose a local file** and select your own copy of the game HTML. This browser remembers the file, and **Play offline** opens it next time.
