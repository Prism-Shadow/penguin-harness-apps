# Transport Ship

Play the author’s original 3D team game online or import your sound-fixed Transport Ship HTML to play offline.

Open **Transport Ship** in your Agent’s App Center. WASD to move, mouse to look, click to play. Press Esc to pause. For the revised audio, choose Open local HTML and select the sound-fixed game file.

The author’s online game has its original audio. Choose **Open local HTML** and select your sound-fixed Transport Ship HTML to play the revised audio offline. The workbench saves your file in this browser.
