# Harbor Pulse

Tag floating signal buoys from a cargo deck in a 45-second arcade round with original stereo percussion.

Install the plugin on your Agent, then open Harbor Pulse in the App Center.

Click or tap a buoy, or use arrows/WASD and Space. Repeated hits build a combo. Sound check plays four synthetic shots and the hit chime. Pause with Escape. Sound and the high score are saved in this browser.

The game design, canvas art and audio synthesis are original to this showcase. The starting idea came from [cf-transport-ship in riba2534’s three-game demo](https://github.com/riba2534/claude-opus-5-5-demo/tree/54adc1fb64155f9dd7c1c90ecae7b8185ac540b8/cf-transport-ship).
