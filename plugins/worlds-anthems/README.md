# Worlds Anthems

Play eight League of Legends Worlds songs and official arrangements in the visible SoundCloud player. Choose a season, seek, change volume, or open the track on SoundCloud if embedding is unavailable. The Warriors link plays the official 2020 arrangement.

The optional workbench runs in the browser. The official recordings require an Internet connection for streaming. Playback availability depends on SoundCloud and the track publisher.

[中文版](README.zh.md)

The playlist advances when a song finishes. Turn on Repeat playlist to start again after the last anthem. The separate offline channel contains two original PenguinHarness instrumentals, **Northbound** and **Afterglow**. The player renders the bundled scores locally, including their bass lines, arpeggios, and melodies.
